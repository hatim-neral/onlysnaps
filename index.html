The pack

A sachet you fold, not tear.

Ordinary sachets need two hands and usually spill. Ours is scored down the middle. Fold it in half and it snaps open, then pours from one small opening.

Try it: tap the pack

Flat as a card

Each pack is a slim, card-sized sachet holding one drink. It slips into a pocket, a gym bag or a hotel tray.

Scored down the middle

The dashed line is a snap line cut into the stiff side of the pack. It stays sealed until you fold it.

Opens with one hand

Hold the pack between thumb and fingers and fold it in half. It snaps open at the centre. No tearing, teeth or scissors.

Pours where you aim

The drink leaves through one small opening at the fold, in a steady stream, so nothing runs over your fingers.

How to use it

Three moves. Ten seconds. Done.

Every pack holds one shot. There's nothing to measure, brew or wash.

STEP 1

Snap

Hold the pack flat over your glass and fold it in half along the dashed line. It snaps open at the centre.

STEP 2

Pour

Keep squeezing and the shot pours from the fold. Use milk, water or ice, hot or cold.

STEP 3

Sip

Stir once. You have a café-style cold brew, masala chai or iced tea, with no queue.

Where it comes from

Coffee from the hills of Karnataka. Tea from the gardens of Assam.

Our coffee comes from shade-grown estates in the Western Ghats of Karnataka. Our tea comes from the tea gardens of Assam, in the Brahmaputra valley, known for strong, malty black tea. We cold brew the coffee slowly so the shot stays smooth and low in bitterness, and brew the tea strong so it holds up in milk or over ice.

Questions

Good to know.

What's inside a shot?

The coffee shot is cold brew concentrate made from 100% Arabica coffee and water. The masala chai and iced tea shots are tea concentrates. Full ingredients and caffeine details for each are on our spec sheet.

Does it need a fridge?

We share storage and shelf-life details on the spec sheet for each batch, so your team knows exactly how to stock it.

Hot or cold?

Both. Use the coffee or chai shot in hot milk or water for a hot drink, or pour any shot over ice for a cold one.

Can we put our hotel's or company's name on the pack?

Yes. Once a hotel or office orders regularly, we can print your brand on the pack and box. Ask us for the minimum quantity.

Can our office canteen sell it?

Yes. We can supply your canteen directly, or the catering company that runs it.

Where do you deliver?

We start with Mumbai, Lonavala, Alibaug and Karjat, and can ship further for larger orders.
